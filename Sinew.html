Four steps between a thought and a grip.

The loop runs continuously. Each stage hands off to the next in under five milliseconds.

Sense

Eight dry electrodes in the socket pick up the faint voltage your forearm muscles produce when you intend to move.

2,000 samples / s

Decode

A small on-device model separates a pinch from a fist from a relaxed hand, tuned to your own signal during fitting.

< 5 ms

Drive

Six brushless micro-motors move the joints in proportion to how hard you flex, so gentle intent gives a gentle grip.

6 degrees of freedom

Feel

Fingertip pressure feeds back as a light vibration on the skin of your residual limb, so you know what you are holding.

0.1 N resolution
